This record applies the rules in `2026-10-13-election-rerating-rules.md` to the records corrected in `2026-10-13-record-corrections.md`. It moves the five ratings the rules record listed and no others.

| State | Rule | Rating | Fit, Balanced | Rank, Balanced |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Alabama | R3, for HB 399's narrowing | `po` 4 to 3 | 55.3 to 52.2 | 29 to 33 |

Other states shift by one or two places as these four move. The Balanced leaders remain North Dakota, Tennessee, Idaho, Indiana and Michigan.

The Texas change is temporary by rule. When the TCEQ hold and the interconnection hold lift, R7 restores `po` 3 and `op` 3, `zn` returns to 0 and `pg` to 2. ERCOT's report is targeted for December 10, 2026.
